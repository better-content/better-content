# Thread Art Direction

The 52 illustrations share an **impossible archive plate** grammar: illuminated-manuscript composition fused with occult copperplate engraving and industrial technical lithography, with painterly depth inside precise etched linework. They are epic science-fantasy artifacts, not cyberpunk UI and not replicas of any tarot deck.

Every master is a strict 2:3 portrait, full bleed, with safe margins, one readable focal silhouette, and enough large structure to survive 256×384 presentation. Masters contain no frame, title, letters, words, numerals, UI, logo, or watermark. Code owns deterministic framing and typography. Recurring evidence—three braided lines, an off-axis annular cache with three breaks, old classifications, and traces of a second architecture—remains ambiguous; no plate establishes a common origin.

## Four-suit grammar

| Suit | Color | Visual tendency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World | `#66704C` | strata, horizons, weather, routes, ecosystems, encountered realms |
| Works | `#956044` | hands, mechanisms, heat, sequence, infrastructure, useful motion |
| Powers | `#6B6380` | bodies, contracts, syntax, spirits, anchors, transformed rules |
| Fragility | `#805356` | tethers, ruins, divided threats, copies, campaigns, world succession |

The suit color controls the reader edge. It is not a whole-image tint; automatic dust remains shared archive gold.

## Canonical aspect trace

Each plate carries exactly one Systemic Salience aspect as a path, current, fracture, or connection through the composition: Impact `#E4717D`, Tempo `#AA652B`, Work `#CAA903`, Mobility `#C0E304`, Endurance `#35BBD0`, Robustness `#1175FC`, Renewal `#6FEDBA`, or Control `#8A6CB2`. Archive substrate uses gold `#C6A15B`; neutral black and parchment are shade and paper only. Card-unique pigment supplies the third semantic role.

## Runtime art contract

Every approved master produces two 256×384 runtime files:

1. the untouched resized full image;
2. a subdued grayscale catalogue thumbnail.

Unread development uses no generated presentation layers. The reader crossfades the untouched native illustration directly over its neutral code-drawn archive plate for 800 ms. The illustration's authored pigment—including its canonical aspect color—remains part of the art rather than a separate GUI effect.

Masters are generated individually with the built-in image-generation workflow. Each prompt combines this shared grammar, the card-specific scene brief, suit pigment tendency, exact aspect trace, and the no-text/no-frame constraint. The approved tricolor-v1 masters remain anchors for line density, paper, contrast, and restraint; generated candidates remain isolated until the real-client crossfade and completed-reader captures are approved.
