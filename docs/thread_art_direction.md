# Thread Art Direction

The eighteen illustrations use a shared **impossible archive plate** grammar: illuminated-manuscript composition fused with occult copperplate engraving and industrial technical lithography, with painterly depth inside precise etched linework. They are epic sci-fi/fantasy artifacts, not cyberpunk UI and not replicas of any tarot deck.

All production prompts require a strict 2:3 portrait composition, full bleed, safe margins, a strong focal silhouette, and readability at small card size. Generated masters contain no frame, title, letters, words, numerals, UI, logo, or watermark. Game code supplies deterministic framing and typography.

Recurring evidence is deliberately ambiguous: three braided lines, an off-axis annular cache with three breaks, old classifications, and traces of a second architecture. No card establishes whether these share an origin.

## Card briefs

- **The World Remembers:** stratified ground preserves overlapping footprints, a note marker, and a death echo along a path toward an erased horizon.
- **A Life Becomes Capable:** empty travel armor beneath a seven-branch constellation tree whose lines continue into successor silhouettes.
- **Feast Before the Journey:** an expedition feast and ration store cast the impossible shadow of an hourglass-ledger machine.
- **Stone Makes Promises:** an ore-body cutaway transforms into assays, retorts, tanks, and dirty chemical conduits.
- **Materials Have Temperaments:** materially distinct tool behaviours face an unknown hand mirroring the player's choice through an archive layer.
- **Motion Becomes Industry:** one colossal shaft drives a settlement that continues working beneath an advancing unnatural storm.
- **A Vessel Can Become a Place:** a room-scale habitat vessel leaves a coastline dissolving into cartographic fragments.
- **Rails Turn Distance into Machinery:** a network is simultaneously landscape and strategic map as industrial and military routes intersect.
- **Pressure Changes Matter:** nested sealed chambers compress stone, vapor, and stellar light through cracked strata.
- **Machines Can Remember:** a silent storage reliquary surrounds a dark cache and an empty operator station.
- **Doors Borrow Other Worlds:** a charged aperture borrows an alien landscape while three tethers drain it homeward.
- **Flight Must Be Engineered:** player-made flight climbs through older, unfamiliar flight traces in a layered sky.
- **An Army Walks Toward You:** directional armies and another apocalypse collide beyond a small fort whose location is not the world's center.
- **Leave the Atmosphere:** one vertical ascent joins factory, atmosphere, orbit, and a destination bearing ancient landing evidence.
- **Blood Can Become Infrastructure:** a crimson ritual-fluid city and an unfamiliar pale machine implement analogous economies without gore.
- **Reality Has Grammar:** nonlinguistic spell clauses alter a landscape over older, half-erased corrections.
- **Spirits Honour Contracts:** four incompatible ritual authorities answer one request through different materials and rules.
- **A World Can Be Inherited:** a condenser transfers a small cache into a successor while a second cache remains unnoticed in the dying world.

The source masters were generated individually with the built-in image-generation workflow after three anchors—World Remembers, Motion Becomes Industry, and Reality Has Grammar—established the shared treatment. Each remaining prompt combined this shared contract with its card brief and palette; future replacements must follow the same anchor-and-review process.

## Tricolor development contract

Review masters use exactly three semantic roles: archive substrate `#C6A15B`, one canonical Systemic Salience trace, and one card-specific pigment. Neutral black and parchment are shade and paper only. The trace is a path, current, fracture, or connection through the composition, never a whole-image tint.

The runtime review set is downsampled to 256×384. Deterministic CIE Lab distance masks derive an opaque archive-sepia structure layer, a transparent unique-pigment layer, and a transparent aspect-trace layer. Those masks exist only for development: after the third pass the reader displays the untouched full runtime image, so mask generation cannot reduce final fidelity.
